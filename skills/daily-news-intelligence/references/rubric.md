# Search Scope

- Search the requested date, country, and category.
- China: foreign media only; exclude Chinese domestic media and government domains.
- Europe: Europe-ex-UK. Exclude UK-primary events, but allow UK outlets covering eligible European events.
- Scanner: one broad search, one optional low-coverage refinement, at most 10 results, no quota. Remove exact duplicates and favor different events.
- Verifier: no web; keep the first same-event report, preserve category, and retain at most 6 unique events per category. Keep all when fewer exist.
- Do not require article opening, exact-date revalidation, readable body, publisher tier, byline, provenance, numeric scoring, Lead replacement, rerouting, or filler.
- Treat distinct follow-ups, reactions, decisions, and transaction stages as separate events.
- Scanner and Verifier defects are advisory; preserve usable content and continue with documented fallbacks.
